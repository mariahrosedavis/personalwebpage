How Home Budget works

01

Set your comfort level

The calculator checks both your housing-only budget and your total housing-plus-debt budget.

02

Include the overlooked costs

Taxes, insurance, HOA fees, mortgage insurance, and maintenance are included in the monthly picture.

03

Experiment freely

For planning only. This calculator is not a loan approval, lending offer, financial advice, or guarantee of affordability. Debt ratios use gross income, not take-home pay. Allow separately for utilities, living expenses, savings, moving costs, and emergency funds. Taxes can change after purchase, and insurance may require separate wind or flood coverage. PMI is an illustrative conventional-loan estimate below 20% down; FHA, VA, and other programs differ.
